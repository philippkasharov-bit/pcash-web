# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

International clients — startups, small businesses, and founders — looking for a freelance partner who handles design, code, and AI integration without subcontracting. Bilingual audience (Russian-first, English supported).

## Product Purpose

pcash.pro is Philipp Kasharov's freelance portfolio and lead-generation site. It sells web development, UX audit, and AI-bot integration services. Success = qualified inbound leads through the contact form.

## Positioning

Full-cycle solo operator: design, frontend code, and AI automation delivered by one person with no handoffs, fixed pricing, fixed timelines, and live progress visibility. Competitors either specialize in one layer or run a team with coordination overhead.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Static HTML/CSS/JS site served via a simple dev server (no framework)
- Bilingual i18n via data-ru / data-en attributes with a JS language switcher
- Three.js wireframe background animation, GSAP + ScrollTrigger scroll effects, Lenis smooth scroll
- Contact form submits to a backend endpoint
- Chat demo widget (scripted, labelled as demo)
- 13 real portfolio products in the catalog

## Brand Commitments

- Name: P.Cash (stylized P.Cash with orange dot)
- Voice: direct, honest, no corporate jargon — "if the task isn't mine, I'll say so"
- Language: Russian primary, English toggle
- Colors: deep purple/noir background, orange accent, violet secondary, green for positive states

## Evidence on Hand

- Two published case studies (KRSH e-commerce, P.Cash portfolio site)
- Real project screenshots in the showcase browser mockup
- No fabricated testimonials or metrics — facts only (3 years AI experience, 12+ projects launched)

## Product Principles

1. Honesty over polish — never fabricate credentials, testimonials, or metrics
2. One person, zero handoffs — the value proposition is the absence of coordination overhead
3. Show, don't tell — case studies and live demos prove competence better than claims
4. Respect the visitor's time — fixed pricing and clear process reduce decision friction
5. Bilingual by default — every surface works in both Russian and English

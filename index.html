About me

I came to the web through AI — and stayed for the result

I'm 22. I started with AI — prompting, automations, working through new tools as they appeared. It became clear fairly quickly that applying the technology to someone's problem is more interesting than studying it.

Over three years I built up the full cycle: interface, markup, code, launch. I deliberately keep the service list short — I take on what I can do end to end myself, with no subcontractors.

Honestly about experience: there are no corporate projects or big names behind me — there is freelance work delivered for clients. I work directly: no managers, no middlemen, nothing passed down a chain. If I can see a job is not mine, I say so straight away.

  • AI is my focusBots and automation are my strongest area — and rarer to find in a web freelancer.
  • Full cycleDesign, code and launch in one pair of hands — no «I'll hand it to a coder» and no quality lost at the seams.
  • Honest and transparentI fix price and deadline in writing and show progress along the way. First clients get below-market pricing for an honest review.
